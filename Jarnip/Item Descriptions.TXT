Cursed Berserking Sword +5: Bloodlust

Waterdhavian parents often tell their children the tale of Bloodlust: a cursed berserking sword with an insatiable desire to murder that ultimately turns on its wielder to quench its thirst for blood. The moral of the story is to never accept unknown magical items from strangers, and the sword was thought to be nothing more than a legend. However, it now lies before you.

The crimson blade is covered in bloodmarks, suggesting it is hundreds of years old. If the legends are true, it has caused the death of thousands, and countless innocents have fallen victim to its malevolent influence. The origins of Bloodlust are unclear, but it is evident that it was created for one purpose: to kill without end and unleash carnage across the Realms. 

The curse of this sword permanently marks the wielder: their hair turns white and begins to fall out, while their skin becomes ghastly and riddled with blisters. Over time, they lose physical fortitude and wither from within. The only way to alleviate this affliction is through satiating the blade's desire for bloodshed, yet the curse itself may never be lifted.

Once equipped, this cursed sword cannot be removed or unequipped by any known spell or ritual. The wielder has no choice but to obey and continue the slaughter. Only death can grant freedom from its curse.

STATISTICS:

Equipped abilities:
- Cursed
- Immune to Remove Curse
- Charisma: -5 penalty
- Constitution: -2 penalty
- Appearance changes: skin turns pale gray, hair becomes bone-white

Combat abilities:
- Murderous Wrath: On each hit, the wielder must Save vs. Death at -2 or go berserk for 2 rounds. When berserk, the movement rate is doubled and the base number of attacks per round is set to 5
- Dread Hunger: On each hit, the target must Save vs. Death or receive extra 2d4 magic damage. The stolen Hit Points are transferred to the wielder. This will not heal beyond the wielder's maximum
- Bloodlust: Killing an enemy restores 4d6 Hit Points and increases Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution by 1 for 1 turn

THAC0: +5
Damage: 2d4+5 (slashing)
Speed Factor: 3
Proficiency Type: Bastard Sword
Type: One-handed
Requires:
 10 Strength

Weight: 10

Price: 30,000 gp

This exceptional leather coat once belonged to Herbert Schwarz, a famous Waterdhavian duelist whose adventures along the Sword Coast became the subject of innumerable folk tales. Herbert was known for being able to escape from even the most dangerous situations, a talent many attributed to his magic armor. Durable, lightweight, and enchanted with a permanent blessing of fortune, this coat is the dream of any adventurer.

STATISTICS:

Equipped abilities:
- Saving Throws: +1
- Luck: +1 (Swashbucklers)

Combat abilities:
- Avoid Death: When the wearer's Hit Points fall below 10%, grants 15 temporary hit points, +4 to Armor Class, and +4 to Saving Throws for 1 round

Armor Class: 2 (0 vs. slashing, 1 vs. piercing and missile), 1 (Swashbucklers)
Requires:
 8 Strength

Weight: 10

Price: 20,000 gp

Nethraval's Ardor
The legendary eldritch knight Nethraval wore this enchanted circlet during his final battle, when he descended into Cania to rescue his betrothed from the captivity of Mephistopheles. The outcome of their confrontation remains unknown, but somehow the circlet survived and eventually found its way back to Toril.

STATISTICS:

Equipped abilities:
- Saving Throws vs. Spell: +2
- Magic Damage Resistance: +30%
- Can memorize one extra arcane 4th-level spell, one extra 5th-level spell, and one extra 6th-level spell (Fighter/Mages)
- Protects against critical hits

Weight: 1

Price: 18,000 gp

Eternal Torment +3
The intricate ornamentation suggests that this dagger was originally crafted in ancient Illefarn for some kind of religious ritual. However, whatever magic this blade once possessed has since been corrupted by the lich Velsharoon, who used it to sacrifice countless victims in an attempt to achieve godhood. Whether the dagger was stolen from Velsharoon or discarded after he found a more powerful artifact, the necromantic curse it possesses is as sinister as it is powerful.

STATISTICS:

Equipped abilities:
- Cursed
- Magic Resistance: -20% penalty
- Save vs. Death: +2
- Caster Level: +2 (Necromancers)
- Can memorize one extra arcane 6th-level spell, one extra 7th-level spell, and one extra 8th-level spell (Necromancers)

Charge abilities:
- Summon five skeleton warriors twice per day

THAC0: +3
Damage: 1d4+3 (piercing), +1 (cold)
Speed Factor: 0
Proficiency Type: Dagger
Type: One-handed
Requires:
 3 Strength

Weight: 1

Price: 25,000 gp

Dance of Death +4
Few know the true name of the assassin who committed a series of murders targeting high-profile figures in Athkatla some fifty years ago. The surviving accounts call her only the Dancer, a master of stealth who could slip through crowded streets and guarded halls without ever being seen. It is said that she served no lord and accepted no payment, targeting wealthy slavers and corrupt officials according to her own sense of justice. After her final assassination of a high-ranking Cowled Wizard, the Dancer was nearly captured but somehow vanished, leaving behind only her blade.

STATISTICS:

Equipped abilities:
- Backstab Multiplier: -1 penalty
- Non-detection
- Hide in Shadows and Move Silently: +15%

Combat abilities:
- Dance of Death: On a successful backstab, the target must Save vs. Death at -2 or die (Shadowdancers)

THAC0: +4
Damage: 1d6+4 (piercing)
Speed Factor: 1
Proficiency Type: Short Sword
Type: One-handed
Requires:
 5 Strength

Weight: 2

Price: 30,000 gp
